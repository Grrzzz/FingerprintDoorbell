WiFi setup
You are currently in WiFi configuration mode!

In this mode the device acts as a WiFi access point to which you can temporarily connect with your smartphone/PC to configure the access to your regular WiFi network.

After saving the new WiFi configuration the device will restart and try to connect to your network. If this fails the device will not go back to WiFi config mode automatically. You have to put the device manually in WiFi config mode by pressing and holding the sensor surface for 10 s while powering up the device.
